# Battle System Design

## Purpose

This document records the game rules of the Battle component (`internal/core/battle`): legacy evidence, approved decisions and open gaps.

Battle is an independent, reusable domain component. It resolves combat between participants and determines outcomes without knowing why the battle was started (e.g., adventure quest, arena, dungeon, boss, or guild battle).

Participant, action, field and request shapes, implemented formulas and log text are owned by the code and tests under `internal/core/battle` and `internal/battle`; this document does not repeat them.

Evidence paths are under `party2/lib/` at revision `ff97fc549932a146cc945d7edf71b44c868a840e` unless stated otherwise. Numeric item, gem and job references are provenance identifiers, not authorization to reuse distinctive display names.

## Batch resolver

The batch resolver (`ResolvePartyBattle`) is the automatic, agility-ordered resolver used by non-interactive combat modes. It is not the original command-by-command normal-adventure combat (`_battle.cgi`, `_skill.cgi`); see [Adventure](adventure.md#original-interaction-contract-and-reconstruction-target). Reconstruction reuses verified combat effects, but not the batch action selection, agility round order or automatic termination as the interaction rule.

Legacy rules the batch resolver preserves:

- Virulent poison (劇毒) deals 10% MaxHP (capped at 950–1049 when >999) to every other afflicted living party member on each ally action (`_battle.cgi:792–807`), then to the afflicted actor (`_battle.cgi:810`); no natural cure.
- Deadly poison (猛毒) deals the same damage to the actor at the end of its own turn (`_battle.cgi:810`); no natural cure.
- Poison (毒) deals the same damage at the end of the actor's turn and then cures at 1/5 if the actor is alive (`_battle.cgi:840`).

Known differences of the batch resolver, outside the explicit-action reconstruction:

- Revival uses `CheckRevival`'s ability-based approximation (fixed undying recovery, quarter-HP item193, trigger consumption). The explicit engine uses [the ordered revival contract](#resources-revival-and-roster-changes).
- The 100-iteration draw is a safeguard, not a legacy termination rule; an interactive encounter must not inherit it or any other fictional operation timeout.
- Cursed actors skip current/previous-job and custom skills without MP/CMP debit. Holding item249 applies the per-entry magician-name classification (`_battle.cgi:764–780,1804–1825`; `_skill.cgi:68–77,263–273`) rather than halving every magic-category skill.

Magic rules shared with the explicit engine (`_skill.cgi:8811–8823,9081–9135,9954–10144`):

- Magic attacks use direct base power without physical attack scaling and bypass defense. Item149 (×1.1 direct) skips the tension multiplier and keeps tension; item143 doubles (truncated) when power <300; a `kinju` target takes ×1.25; item144 scales area hits ×1.3 when modified power <300. Tension is consumed on the first effective hit. Successive area targets decay ×0.85 unless item144 is held with initial power ≤300.
- Magic healing applies item146 (×1.8), item256 (×1.2 plus living-ally splash capped at 30% of the primary target's MaxHP), caster `sabaku` (×0.7–0.9) and target virulent-poison truncation `int(v×0.1)` before variance and tension, then resets tension to 1 regardless of item149.
- A magic category seal cures at 25%; on failure MP is still debited and the effect aborts without consuming tension.

### Shared continuation rules

These rules apply to every callback family below; sections do not repeat them.

- **Whole-effect admission.** An action is available only when its whole reachable effect has an evidenced native implementation: callback-specific targets, resources, resets, death/revival, roster mutations and durable side effects. Learning, shop or synthesis availability does not certify a battle effect, and shared helper presence does not certify concrete entries. Unknown effects never become ordinary attacks; an empty custom action never spends CMP. The gate applies to player, helper and ordinary-enemy actions, including random or delegated invocation. Missing behavior remains a cutover prerequisite, never a permanent omission.
- **Preflight.** The enclosing operation preflights every reachable continuation before cost, RNG or mutation. A missing descendant rejects the enclosing (including indirect) choice without narrowing its original draw pool. Exact, unconditional writes may project into later admission; unknown transitions keep the conservative gate. A supported operation with no eligible target is an accepted no-effect action and is charged normally.
- **Ordered tables.** Job tables keep every original position, including positions owned by other sections (wallet, inspection, field, book, revival, arithmetic). Registering one family never overwrites another family's binding.
- **Costs.** Listed ordinary costs belong to the invoking consumer and are charged after the callback, including accepted no-effect and caster death, unless the callback reports `$mes`. Random/tool MP restoration and delegated extended debits follow [random and delegated invocation](#random-and-delegated-job-closure).
- **Persistence.** All behaviorally relevant facts survive serialize/reload: ordered participants, resources, item instances/consumption, banishment, crystal drops, temporary/extended effects, tension/hit, CMP recharge, room skill bans, per-side history and temporary/default/anti fields. Persist encounter identities and randomized outcomes already produced; retries read committed results rather than rerolling. Save the frozen input and resulting facts with the consumer's receipt, rebuild runtime ports on reload, and return the saved receipt on retry without re-executing callbacks. Neither the RNG nor deferred executable work is saved.

### Operation boundaries

Player incapacitation consumes the accepted operation but skips the normal-action block; a paralysis/sleep cure also skips it. Player other-ally virulent poison precedes actor poison; ordinary NPCs omit that party pass. Suppression does not bypass normal-action poison.

Unaccepted operations are inert; accepted incapacitated actions can still reach field/player tails. Player field ticks precede the tail and need neither a normal action nor a living actor; anti-field ticks even without enemies. The player counter/end-processing tail additionally requires remaining enemies, no suppression/NPC comment and no preceding level-up branch. NPC response has its own purification/end-processing tail and never ticks the player's fields/CMP/bans/incense again. Damage-helper invocation counts are recorded at the original helper points, including misses and single-use defense, not inferred from HP deltas. Additional-action parsing captures effect eligibility before the first callback. CMP checks the saved threshold before incrementing.

Normal-action temporary expiry preserves the player mirror-sharing/item163 exceptions and consumes the original random draw even for empty temporary state. Reflection/cover redirects persist; only one-hit defense is consumed at its damage-helper point. Reset restores tension1/hit95 and clears status/effects, then reapplies current equipment separately for requester, actor and target; a cached encounter-start profile cannot replace equipment callbacks. HP/MP/CMP, consumed items, bans and history survive reset. Explicit advance performs the first living item188 holder's ally revival pass, resets members with current equipment, reapplies living holders' revival temporary effect, and clears temporary/anti fields while retaining the default.

**Player equipment tails.** These passive effects are separate from submitted commands and random/job tables. Item210 captures fallen party records before the action; its recovery runs after revival even when the combat tail is skipped. Item189 belongs to the living normal-action block; its chance draw precedes the suppression check. Other player equipment effects require the eligible combat tail; living-only effects precede CMP and extended effects. Item194 resets through current equipment, resolves half the stat-sum difference as untyped direct damage or healing, then restores captured tension/status/temporary/hit while keeping reset stats and extended state. Items202/198 use tool recovery's optional temporary hook and can restore zero; they do not use spell recovery's tension or MP exclusions.

**Shared player/NPC equipment tails** (`_battle.cgi:607–613,862–921,984–1038,1097–1134`; `_npc_action.cgi:161–168,366–491`). Capture before status/action callbacks: item206 remembers an equipped holder's nonempty status, item246 remembers any original temporary. Item164 clears the current temporary before its quarter-chance tension call; item209's covering-only 3% tool heal follows. Field burn/grave/library and player field ticks sit between these boundaries and item206's cleared-status response. Item206 temporarily sets tension1 for its 30% untyped heal and independent half-chance direct attack, then restores tension even after defeat. These outer player boundaries require no suppression and need neither enemies, a normal action nor a living actor; NPC equivalents sit outside the normal-action block without player guards.

- NPC item202 requires an empty current temporary and tool-heals 3–5% MaxHP; it never uses the player's half-HP/4%/temporary-hook formula.
- Item208 counts the full ordered same-side roster, including fallen members: at 3 it cures exact statuses on afflicted holders and raises every hit below 95; at 4 it makes one untyped direct 10%-AT hit per member with tension temporarily 1; at 5 it tool-heals each member 10% MaxHP capped 200; at 6 it attempts tension for every member at 1/3. Repeated hits keep reset/defeat/one-use-defense continuations and read current attack each time.
- The guardian's presence in its side's roster, even fallen, tool-heals the actor 0.5–3.5% MaxMP. Player guardian requires a living actor and precedes item208; NPC guardian follows item208.
- Item246 installs one of four category reflections only for a living holder without an original temporary. Player item208/246 require the eligible combat tail; player item246 follows extended/incense effects; NPC item246 precedes purification/specter.

CMP recharge uses the requester's level capped at 99. Frenzy's random member tool hit and crown's probabilistic tool heal run before completion; frenzy expiry clamps attack to 999 even for a fallen actor.

End processing captures the first qualifying darkness debit (floor of half the damage, capped 500), filters it against immediate risk invocation facts once before flushing, skips fallen actors and leaves living actors at least 1 HP. Gems101/102 have no valid reachable queued execution and stay wholly unsupported, including their enclosing closures.

### Dynamic effects and catalog admission

Definitions map catalog IDs to validated native payloads; learning or commerce definitions cannot create combat entries by matching kind strings. Occupied custom slots hydrate in original order, including duplicates, only when every slot passes; otherwise the whole custom choice is omitted. Discovery and submission derive from the same server-owned definitions; client-submitted payloads are never trusted.

NPC action selection starts with the mode's SP0/MP0 normal attack (`vs_monster.cgi:11–14`), independently of job0's five SP-gated positions. It builds current/previous-job lists with independent gates and custom gems (`_npc_action.cgi:158–322`), then randomly selects an admitted position. NPC-only tables need their own complete bodies even when they share player helpers. Selection does not reuse the player's preferred-action policy or equipment extension list. Paralysis and sleep cures skip action on that operation. The special boss-field and named-field extra cure draws are separate branches. NPC poison/regeneration/rage run inside the normal-action block (`324–361`); item/field/ability tails outside it (`364–495`) can still run after an incapacitated action. Burn is nonlethal, capped at 200, and clears temporary/status/extended state; a grave-field helper can be restored or recreated. These tails are not one generic “end turn” hook with the same eligibility for every actor.

**Jobs88/90–94** (`_skill.cgi:7043–7178`), nineteen positions. Job88's physical immobilization and armed/unarmed direct claw use the shared helpers. ## Other combat rules

- **Agility exceed check (`_is_exceed_ag`).** Standard: 1/3 (`rand(3)==0`) and rand(actor AG) ≥ rand(target AG×3). Item147: 1/2 and rand(actor AG) ≥ rand(target AG). It is used for both the critical roll (actor vs target) and the evasion roll (target vs actor).
- **Cursed actors** cannot choose or execute custom or current/previous-job skills (`_skill.cgi:35–36,84–85,293`).
- **Weapon71** base attack is int(mat×(0.75+rand0.5)), or int(mat×(1.5+rand0.5)) with item158 or awakening gems 240–242 in the accessory slot; level factor 0.5 below 50, 0.7 below 75, otherwise 1.0 (`_battle.cgi:1736–1744`). Weapons69/70 add int(min(level,99)×1.5) / ×2.0 (`_battle.cgi:1728–1734`).
- **Awakening gem accessories** (`_data.cgi:1997–1999`): item240 +30 AT/−20 DF/−20 AG; item241 −20 AT/+30 DF/−20 AG; item242 −20 AT/−20 DF/+30 AG.
- **Monster kills** (`kill_m`): on victory, each surviving character counts every defeated non-player enemy that is strong relative to the player (`_battle.cgi:230–235`). The demon-king unseal counter is driven by the explicit [stage19 treasure event](adventure.md#combat-travel-and-encounter-replacement), not by victory; see [Boss unsealing](boss.md#6-unseal-to-sealing-room-lifecycle).
- **Damage floor scope.** The physical base formula has a 1-or-2 fallback. Misses, evasion, immunity and later modifiers can yield zero; it is not a minimum for every attack or skill.
- **Context isolation.** The battle engine never queries persistence or mutates character state; feature modules persist results through `internal/battle`.
